# CODING.md

Coding standards for sprezzature-ux-laws.

Key rules: NumPy-style docstrings on every function, full typing
(`from __future__ import annotations` at the top of every file),
roughly 25-30% comment density explaining *why* a non-obvious line
does what it does, ruff-clean (`ruff check .`), no bare `except
Exception` without a comment explaining why the broad catch is
intentional.

Stdlib only in the checks of `scripts/audit_laws_of_ux.py`: no
`beautifulsoup`, no `lxml`. The auditor parses HTML with
`html.parser.HTMLParser` on purpose, to stay dependency-free and fast
enough for a pre-commit hook. Its command line parses with click, the
one parser of the suite and a base dependency, imported inside `main()`
of the auditor script. The library is meant to import without click
(`tests/test_the_library_and_the_twin.py` checks it), but today
`import sprezzature_ux_laws` loads click through `_skill.py` (the skill
command, shared verbatim with the other bricks), which imports it at module
level: that test is red until `_skill.py` is fixed in the hub.

`sprezzature_ux_laws/_skill.py` and `tests/test_the_skill_ships_with_the_package.py`
are synced from the sprezzature hub by its `scripts/sync_helpers.py`, as is
`scripts/_click.py`: change them there, not here. The skill itself lives in
`sprezzature_ux_laws/skill/`; its `references/triggers.md` and the
`description:` of its `SKILL.md` are generated by the hub's
`scripts/build_triggers.py`.
